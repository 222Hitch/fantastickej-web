# A Morning Routine That Takes Less Than 5 Minutes

Long morning routines full of meditation, exercise, and journaling look great on social media, but in reality most people never stick to them. The good news is that even a five-minute routine can kick-start your day far better than reaching straight for your phone.

## Minute one: a glass of water

The body is dehydrated after sleep, and water right after waking up helps jump-start both metabolism and mind.

## Minutes two to three: a few stretches

It doesn't have to be a full workout — a few stretching moves are enough to get blood flowing and shake off morning stiffness.

## Minute four: one positive sentence

Instead of scrolling through notifications right after waking up, try a short motivational phrase instead — ideally one you have read aloud rather than just glance at. It helps set the tone for the rest of the day before anything else gets the chance to.

## Minute five: one clear first task

Decide what the first thing you'll do today will be. It doesn't have to be anything major, just one concrete step that moves you forward.

## Why it works

The key isn't the length of the routine, but doing it consistently. A short, repeatable routine has a better chance of sticking than an ambitious hour-long plan that falls apart after a week.
